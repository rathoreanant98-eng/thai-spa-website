Before Your Visit

Calm starts with clarity.

Practical guidance for a comfortable, professional visit—with business-specific payment, cancellation and facility rules clearly marked for final confirmation.

Spa Guide

Everything worth knowing before you arrive.

Arrive a little before your appointment so check-in and preferences can be completed without rushing. The final recommended arrival window should be confirmed by the spa.

Wear whatever is comfortable for your visit. Appropriate draping and privacy should be maintained throughout treatments.

Keep voices and devices quiet in relaxation areas so every guest can enjoy a calm environment.

Bring only what you need. The business should confirm how valuables and personal belongings are stored during treatments.

Tell your therapist about pressure, temperature, aroma, areas to avoid and any comfort preferences before or during your session.

The spa maintains a professional environment. Inappropriate, abusive or sexual behaviour should result in the session ending immediately.

Steam and heat experiences should be used only where offered and according to on-site guidance. Stop if you feel unwell or uncomfortable.

If you are pregnant, have a medical condition, are recovering from an injury or are unsure whether a treatment is appropriate, consult an appropriate healthcare professional before booking.

A light meal and normal hydration are generally more comfortable before a spa visit. The business should provide any treatment-specific guidance.

You are encouraged to speak up during the treatment. Pressure and comfort can be adjusted at any time.

Accepted payment methods and any deposit requirements should be confirmed by the business before launch.

The business should confirm whether gratuity is accepted and how it is handled.

Cancellation timing, deposits, late-arrival rules and refund terms must be supplied by the business owner before this policy is published as final.
